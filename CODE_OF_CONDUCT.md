# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want Work Platform to be a practical, respectful, and inclusive project for contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels.

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, architecture, and evidence.
- Assume good intent while still being clear about risks and regressions.
- Respect project scope, module boundaries, and maintainers' review decisions.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or conduct.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions after maintainers have redirected the thread.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, or restrict participation when behavior harms the project or community.

To report a concern, use the security/contact process in `SECURITY.md` or contact the maintainers through the repository owner account.
